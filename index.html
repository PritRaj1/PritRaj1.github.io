Personal research

KAEM: Kolmogorov-Arnold Energy Model

Generative models succumb to trade-offs. GANs sacrifice stability, VAEs sample quality, diffusion speed. KAEM addresses all three.

Design Principles

Representation first

Attention encodes very little inductive bias. Bespoke models win where data is limited.

Intelligence is hierarchical

Good mechanics can compensate for bad software. Good software cannot compensate for bad mechanics.

Reasoning under uncertainty

All models are wrong. The uncaught 1% is where uncertainty quantification saves lives.

Question the default

Diffusion destroys the structure of your target. Annealing keeps it intact.
